Getting started

  1. Install Meshline on two or more iPhones and allow Bluetooth.
  2. Open the People tab. Nearby Meshline users appear under Nearby now.
  3. To save someone, tap Add or check someone in person and scan each other's QR code.
  4. Tap a person to send a private, end to end encrypted message, or tap New group to make a private group.
  5. The Chats tab has a public chat for everyone in range.

Tips

  • Range is about 10 to 100 meters per hop. Messages hop through other Meshline phones, so more people nearby means longer reach.
  • Messages to someone out of range wait and are delivered when a path appears.
  • Keep Meshline open or in the background with Bluetooth on.
